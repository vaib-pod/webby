FROM OUR JOURNAL

Finding focus in a noisy digital world

Modern websites are full of small type, tightly packed paragraphs, and decorative motion that can make reading unnecessarily difficult. Visitors should not have to rediscover the same settings every time they open a new page.

With a personal accessibility profile, WEBBY can remember a comfortable font size and line spacing. On some pages, contrast needs extra attention. On others, reducing motion may be more important.

Use the extension popup to change the page's appearance, then give feedback so the next visit begins with your preferred settings. This demonstration intentionally does not contain login or payment fields.

“Don't adapt yourself to the website. Let the website adapt to you.”

PROTOTYPE VALIDATION

Try it with WEBBY

  1. Open the WEBBY extension popup and enable WEBBY.
  2. Increase text size to 125%, line height to 1.75×, and enable high contrast.
  3. Enable Reduce motion and check that the floating card stops.
  4. Press Tab to move focus; focus outlines should be visible.
  5. Use “Text too small” feedback, then refresh to verify site-specific memory.
  6. Optionally enable AI consent and choose Analyze; observe validated recommendations.
